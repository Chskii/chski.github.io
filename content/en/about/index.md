Hello! My name is Edson. I graduated from the Peruvian University of Applied Sciences (UPC) and I’m passionate about continuing to grow in the world of cybersecurity.
One of the most effective ways I’ve found to reinforce what I learn is to write down and document everything I do. That’s why I decided to create this blog—a space where I’ll share write-ups of systems I’ve compromised, topics I’ve researched, and notes from my training as a pentester and ethical hacker. If you have any suggestions, project ideas, or just want to share your thoughts, you’re more than welcome!
Something I’ve noticed along the way is that many write-ups and walkthroughs assume the reader is already familiar with certain concepts or terminology, which can be a real barrier for those just getting started. My goal with this blog is precisely to break down that barrier: to bring together labs, challenges, and topics in one place, explained clearly and accessibly, without having to consult dozens of external sources to understand what’s being discussed.
